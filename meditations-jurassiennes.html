Sapo Dorado is an artistic and research project led by artists and graphic designers Marion Micigolski and Léonie Monjarret Benoit. Situated at the crossroads of art, graphic design, and science communication, the project explores the current biodiversity crisis and its consequences for living organisms through a visual and poetic approach rooted in the Jura Mountains (France). "La grotte est un refuge dont on rêve sans fin" (The cave is a refuge we dream of endlessly) is a work that serves as a progress report on the research, supplemented by textual contributions from Cosma Cazé and Val Aubois-Liogier.

My contribution for the project Sapo Dorado is a serie of seven poetic and critical texts called Méditations jurassiennes (Jura Meditations).

I performed a reading of Méditations jurassiennes at the artist-runned gallery A (Little) More Careful in Besançon (France), where the publication was edited (by Ride This Book) and presented.
