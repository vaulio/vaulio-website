Val·entin
Aubois-Liogier

Artist & Researcher (Ph.D.)
Based in Paris

Coming from a design background (holding several master's degrees in Design), Val·entin navigate across disiplines and media: performing, designing, writing short texts and poetry, dancing and creating installations. Their work explores socioecological and sociosensory transformations of inhabited spaces, engagin with the banal, liminal and abject. Val did a practice-based doctoral research (defended in July 2026) which focuses on changes in perception and representation in relation with new systems for recycling human urine in agriculture, with a focus on urine collection devices. They also teach in design, art and urban planning at the university through courses, workshops and lectures.
