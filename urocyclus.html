Urocyclus

2021-2023 - Part of my PhD Research

The agricultural recycling of human urine could be a answer to contemporary environmental challenges, such as ecosystem pollution and the waste of energy and resources. However, its implementation poses problems, particularly from a social perspective, as it relates to a daily and ingrained practice: urinating in the toilet. This is what I studied for my doctoral research.

The Urocyclus project, for its part, is a research project that aims to test a device designed for collecting human urine and to conduct field surveys with users and stakeholders to consider the social acceptability of the device based on their sensory experiences.

The urine collection device Urocyclus consists of a gender-neutral, rolled, folded, and welded stainless steel dry urinal attached to an OSB box connected to a reservoir container. It was deployed in two cubicles in March 2023 for testing at the Tanneurs site of the University of Tours and to conduct field surveys.

1:1 scale prototype of the device, produced with the support of the MSH Val de Loire and CITERES laboratory.
