Degré 0 / Cringeness

2025-2026 - Ecole Boulle

Archive pictures of the art class I led last year for the 2nd year of the spatial design bachelor Agencement at Ecole Boulle, pushing to engage the body in spatial practices. A major dimension far too often forgoten.

To lauch and dive into the project CRINGENESS, I did a 45-minutes-long performance called Degré 0 which was exploring through simple score the notion of cringe.

What is cringe? Where does it start? Were does it end? How does this notion limit experimentation? How to reclaim that notion?
