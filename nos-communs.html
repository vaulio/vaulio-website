Nos communs ?

2026 - Design en Recherche

Mapping the results of the “Nos communs ? Enquête interne sur nos pratiques de recherche en design” internal survey on the design research practices of the PhD association Design en Recherche.

“Nos communs” can be read both as “Our commons” (the shared resources, practices, and values of the community) and as “What we have in common” (the bond in the association).

Conducted from March 3 to April 14, 2026. (n=26)

Working group: Valentin Aubois-Liogier, Natalia Baudoin, Anaëlle Beignon, Damien Boisbouvier, Fanny Ehl, Dylan Fluzin, Julia Gonnet, Fanny Maurel, Margaux Moussinet

Data analysis: V. Aubois-Liogier, A. Beignon, D. Fluzin, J. Gonnet, M. Moussinet

Graphic design: V. Aubois-Liogier, D. Fluzin (Banana (Clara Bougon), Recursive (The Recursive Project))
